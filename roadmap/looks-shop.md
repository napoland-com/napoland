A shop, reached from the wardrobe at your chest, that sells looks: outfits, and later jacket patterns and name tag badges. Only looks, never anything that changes play: no energy, no gear, no finds, no XP and no parcels. What you buy is what you see: one look, one price, no game currency and no random rewards. Everyone can still earn looks by playing (outfits with the first sign-in and levels, jacket patterns and badges with merits past level 20), and the shop never sells a look that can be earned.

Why: a way for players who want to support the game to do so, without ever splitting players by power (pillar 1: other players are only ever help). How it is paid for needs a payment provider, terms of sale and a change to the privacy policy, so those come first, when it is scheduled.
