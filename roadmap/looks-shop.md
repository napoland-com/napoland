A shop, reached from the wardrobe at your chest, that sells looks: four outfits, three jacket patterns and three name tag badges of its own. Only looks, never anything that changes play: no energy, no gear, no finds, no XP and no parcels. What you buy is what you see: one look, one price, no game currency and no random rewards. Everyone can still earn looks by playing (outfits with the first sign-in and levels, jacket patterns and badges with merits past level 20), and the shop never sells a look that can be earned. You pay on Stripe's page; the game asks first, with the 14 days you give up for a look you get at once, and links the terms of sale.

It opens once the owner sets up payments (a Stripe account, its keys on the server and the terms of sale: [OPERATIONS.md](../docs/OPERATIONS.md#the-shop)). Until then there is no Shop tab.

Why: a way for players who want to support the game to do so, without ever splitting players by power (pillar 1: other players are only ever help).
