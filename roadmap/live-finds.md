Some finds that turn up only while the woods are restless are live. A live shard is worth 40 XP if you stash it within 4 minutes, then loses 5 a minute until it is a plain shard (12). It glows while you carry it: others see you from far off, and watchers come for you from 12 steps instead of 9. Put it down and it goes dim for good.

Why: the walk home is dead time today; this makes it the tense part of the trip. Lethal Company's apparatus, Death Stranding's cargo that gets damaged.
