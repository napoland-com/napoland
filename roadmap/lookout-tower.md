A wooden fire lookout west of the rocks in the Near Woods, the loggers', older than NAPO (NAPO's Tower stays unclimbable). Climb it from the foot of its ladder and the view pulls back, smoothly, to three times as far, for up to two minutes (B climbs down sooner): the lamp with no wires, the lit shelters, flares going up, the masts' red lights, and far to the south the Old Stone's glow and the Tower's red light, each where it really is and never a marker. Up there is like under a roof (no rain, storms, flashes or hitchhikers, and nothing out there reaches you), but you still tire. Its lamp burns resin, fed at the foot of the ladder (it asks first), 10 minutes each and an hour at most; while someone keeps it fed, its beam sweeps the woods every 20 seconds, a cone everyone sees, sheltering from surges and hitchhikers and freezing watchers where it passes: a light to walk home by, only while it is fed. The notice board says how long it burns.

Why: landmarks to steer by without a marker, and a way to help that is a role of its own. Firewatch, Lethal Company's camera duty.

Later: a lookout in each woods region farther out.
