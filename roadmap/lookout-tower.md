A wooden fire lookout near the rocks in the Near Woods, older than NAPO, from the logging days (NAPO's Tower stays unclimbable), and one in each later woods region. Climb it and the view pulls back to about three times as far, for up to two minutes: the lamp with no wires, the lit shelters, flares going up, the Old Stone's glow and the Tower's red light far to the south. Up there is like under a roof (no rain, storms, flashes or hitchhikers), but you still tire. Later, its lamp burns resin, and while someone feeds it, its beam sweeps the woods every 20 seconds, sheltering from surges and hitchhikers and freezing watchers where it passes: a light to walk home by in a storm, only while it is fed.

Why: landmarks to steer by without a marker, and a way to help that is a role of its own. Firewatch, Lethal Company's camera duty.
