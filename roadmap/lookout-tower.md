A lookout tower on a ridge in the Near Woods (and one per later region). At the top the view pulls back to about three times as far for a while: the Old Stone's glow, the lamp with no wires, lit shelters, flares going up. Its lamp burns resin; while fed, its beam sweeps the woods every 20 seconds, and where it passes it shelters from surges and hitchhikers and freezes watchers, a light to walk home by in a storm.

Why: landmarks to steer by without a marker, and a way to help that is a role of its own (staying up top for the others). Firewatch, Lethal Company's camera duty. Puts light out in the wilds: an owner's decision.
