Hold B (Q on a keyboard) and your character sings a short note in a pitch of its own that carries 25 tiles, heard from the side it comes from and fainter the farther away. Three calls, on a fan that opens above B: short (here I am), long (come here) and double (thank you); slide onto one and let go, or just let go for here I am. The fan says them in words the first few times. Each voice is a note of one pentatonic scale, picked from who you are, so friends learn each other's voices. If the caller is on screen, a little note rises over their head. Anyone may call, guests too: there are no words to filter. A tap on B still opens the bag.

Why: nobody types while walking on a phone, so strangers who meet in the woods cannot work together. Journey's chirp. Sound alone keeps the rule of no position marker, ever: nothing shows at the screen's edge.
