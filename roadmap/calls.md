title: Calls without words
status: idea
order: 150
area: social
---

Hold B to call: your character sings a short note in your jacket color that carries 25 tiles, heard from the side it comes from. Three calls: short (here), long (come), double (thank you).

Why: nobody types while walking on a phone, so strangers who meet in the woods cannot work together. Journey's chirp. Whether anything may show at the screen's edge is an owner's decision (no position marker, ever); sound alone keeps the rule safe.
