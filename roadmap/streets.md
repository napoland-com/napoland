Cabins stand on streets of about 30 neighbors. Streets fill up, and new ones open as players join; friends can live on the same street.

Built: Residents' Lane, thirty cabins reached through the house that was Home in Stonebrook, a copy of it for each street. Out of your cabin you stand in front of your own door; a lit window means a neighbor is at home, their name is on the plate by the door, and A at their door knocks. At your own door you can move next to a friend whose street has a lot free: your cabin comes with you. Visiting inside is for later.

Why: a small neighborhood makes a big world feel like home, and makes it easy to find people to go out with.
