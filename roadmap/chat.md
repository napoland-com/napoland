World chat for everyone online, and local chat for whoever is near you: it shows as a speech bubble over your head, FireRed style, and in the chat log. You reach it from the menu, and the menu button shows a dot when there is something new. Safe from day one: limits on length and speed, a word filter, block and report.

Why: together you go farther, and that starts with talking.

More: [Together: chat and friends](../docs/DESIGN.md#together-chat-and-friends).
