Each place out there where people rest by a fire gets a crate anyone can open: the three shelters of the Near Woods, and on the South Road the NAPO Bunker, the dormitory, the checkpoint and the leavers' camp. It holds 6 things at most; each visit you can leave one thing from your bag and take one out. Every thing says who left it and when ("Resin, left by Ana, 2 h ago"), and taking it thanks them. What comes out of a crate earns no stash XP, so crates cannot be farmed. Gear and tools stay out: a piece of gear carries its own condition, quirk and upgrade.

Why: a new player who reaches a dead fire with no resin is saved by someone who passed hours before, and a high-level player has a use for what they carry spare. Death Stranding's shared lockers.
