Each shelter out in the wilds gets a crate anyone can open: 6 things at most, leave one, take one a visit. It says who left it and when ("left by Ana, 2 h ago"), and taking it thanks them ([thanks](thanks.md)). What comes out of a crate earns no stash XP, so it cannot be farmed.

Why: a new player who reaches a dead fire with no resin is saved by someone who passed hours before, and a high-level player has a use for what they carry spare. Death Stranding's shared lockers.
