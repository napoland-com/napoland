The first player on the server to find each secret (a note, and later a keepsake, a map piece or an echo's walk) gets their name kept with it: a one-line banner for everyone online, the latest three firsts on the notice board, and "first read by Ana, day 3,052" on the page in the journal's field notes of whoever finds it too. Only the first finder is kept. The story's chapters are never secrets: the story is not a race.

Why: discoveries are invisible to the rest of the server today. A name on the board gives players something to talk about and a reason to go where nobody has been, with no competition that costs anyone anything.
