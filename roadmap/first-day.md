A new player hears advice but not what to go for. The chest and the bag show what you could make next with what you have ("Rubber boots: 3 more resin, 1 more scrap"). Mira's lines follow what you have done: before your first stash she points you at resin past the ford and the workbench in the lodge; then she has something to say after your first craft, your first collapse and your first surge. Your first stash and every level get a banner. The first 40 steps into the woods hold a little more resin and glowcaps, so the first trip never comes home empty.

Why: a goal without a mission (no quest log, nothing to fail), so the first five minutes lead into the loop: leave home, gather, come back.
