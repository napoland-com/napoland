A new player always sees one thing worth going for: the chest and the bag show the nearest piece of gear they could make, and what is still missing, counting what the welcome parcel put in the chest ([daily-parcels](daily-parcels.md)): with its 5 resin and 4 cloth, "Next: Rubber gloves. 1 more resin"; when it can be made, they say so, at the workbench at home. The story already points the first steps (chapter 1's hint from Mira, and chapter 2's banner at the first stash). After that, Mira has something to say after your first collapse and your first surge out there, and Walt, in the lodge, about the first thing you made. The first 40 steps into the Near Woods hold more resin and glowcaps, so a first trip never comes home empty. Three small counts are kept for it (the first craft, collapse and surge), like the feats' counts.

Why: a goal without a mission (no quest log, nothing to fail), so the first five minutes lead into the loop: leave home, gather, come back.
