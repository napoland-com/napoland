Come home and put what you carry into your stash: the chest by the fire at home, where each player sees only their own things. Everything you stash earns its XP, once (what you take out and bring back earns nothing again), XP brings levels, and each level makes your energy bar 5 bigger (100 at level 1, 195 at the top, level 20), so you can go farther next time. Your level shows next to your name, and the Status panel in the menu shows your level and XP, and your energy.

Home upgrades wait for [your own cabin](own-cabin.md).

Why: it closes the loop (leave home, gather, come back) and gives every trip a result that lasts.

More: [The loop](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-loop), [Status](../docs/DESIGN.md#status).
