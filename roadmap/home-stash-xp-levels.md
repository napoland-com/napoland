Come home and put what you carry into your stash. Stashing earns XP, XP brings levels, and levels and home upgrades let you go farther next time. A status tab in the menu shows your level and XP, and your energy: the maximum, what you have now and what is draining it at the moment.

Why: it closes the loop (leave home, gather, come back) and gives every trip a result that lasts.

More: [The loop](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-loop), [Status](../docs/DESIGN.md#status).
