A region at depth 2 west of the Near Woods' ring of stones, for one player: the wood the ranger never drew on a map. It looks like one clearing, and it is three that look the same. Each has four ways out; three of them put you back out by the ring of stones, and one leads on into the next clearing, which looks the same as the last. Only the thing in the middle tells them apart: a stump, a cairn by a stone that hums, a row of NAPO's survey stakes.

What is new there:

- **A wood that turns you round.** Nothing is marked and nothing is timed: you find the way by trying, by listening to someone who went through, or by reading the ranger's directions, which she wrote in glowcap ink and which show only at night. The way is fixed, the same for everyone, so it can be shared.
- **The ranger's camp.** Past the third clearing, the camp she kept after she stopped going home: a hut whose fire is always burning when you get there, a kettle still warm, her last notes. Where the ranger went is a little less open, and a new question opens: who keeps her fire going?

Why: a mystery you solve by walking it (Zelda's Lost Woods), told only by what stands there (pillar 1); one fixed world players learn and share (pillar 5). It is the door to [the-other-woods](the-other-woods.md).
