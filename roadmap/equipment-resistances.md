Six slots: cap, shirt, gloves, pants, shoes and bag. Each piece gives resistance to some of the five elements (a raincoat: wind and cold; rubber gloves and boots: electricity), and some give a little extra energy. The bag decides how many slots you carry, from the backpack everyone starts with (8) to an expedition pack (16). Gear comes in tiers, from crafting at the workbench (in the lodge; at home with [workbench-at-home](workbench-at-home.md)) with what you bring home, from rare finds deep in, and from trading with friends. You put it on and take it off at home (and out there too with [gear-on-the-road](gear-on-the-road.md)), it stays with you when you collapse, and others see what you wear. Items stay data in `content/items.json`.

Why: choosing gear is part of planning a trip, and better gear lets you stay out longer and go where the weather would stop you.
