The Ridge's thread is the three who went up the rope and did not all come down. "Nobody comes up this line alone" is on the high hut's wall. At fixed times on the wall clock the see-through figures of the trappers' last climb walk the switchbacks ([old-echoes](old-echoes.md)): three go up, and one of them comes down the next dawn. The footprints the snow already keeps show where they went. At the crest, the last thing they left is something you can only read with someone else standing beside you.

Why: the deepest region should ask the most of you, and have the quietest story. Hollow Knight, Journey.
