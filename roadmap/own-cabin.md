A cabin of your own that only you (and, later, guests you invite) can enter: one of the houses the leavers left, behind your home door, where everyone who walks in is in a cabin of their own. You wake up in it by the fire, facing the room: the first time you play and after every collapse. It holds your chest (your stash, your gear and the wardrobe) and the workbench beside it (at home already: [workbench-at-home](workbench-at-home.md)), and later it can be upgraded ([cabin-comfort](cabin-comfort.md)). Stashing in its chest keeps moving the story, as before.

Why: home is where every trip starts and ends, and upgrading it is part of how you go farther.

More: [World structure](../docs/DESIGN.md#world-structure).
