With a region's map in your hands ([inked-maps](inked-maps.md)), charcoal taken from a burning fire lets you ink in, at a fire, the tiles you walked in that region since the last inking. The walk is kept in memory only; only the inked drawing is saved, never a timed log, and the privacy page says so. Then up to 8 symbols of your own per map, drawn on the paper where you put them.

Why: the map becomes yours, the record of your own trips. The Long Dark's charcoal, Hollow Knight's quill.
