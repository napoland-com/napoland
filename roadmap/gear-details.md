Today one tap on a piece of gear in the chest puts it on, and one tap on what you wear takes it off, so a curious tap changes your gear. From now on a tap only shows what the piece is: its name and tier, what it resists, the energy it adds, how worn it is, its quirk and (once upgrades exist) its level. A double tap puts it on or takes it off, and so does the button in what the tap showed, for anyone who does not know about double taps. The bag already works this way for everything else (a tap opens a slot's details), so the whole game now follows one rule: a tap looks, an action is a second step, and an action that uses something up asks first ([ask-first](ask-first.md)). As built: the card opens in the chest for what you wear and for everything in the stash and your bag (Wear, Take off, Take out, Put away), and at the workbench for each recipe and mend (Make, Mend, with what it takes against what the stash holds); its button, A or a double tap acts, B closes it first; it docks under the list in portrait and beside it in landscape, never over A and B.

Why: gear is the main thing to work toward, and choosing it should never happen by accident. It also makes room for what a piece carries now: condition, quirks, and later upgrades ([gear-upgrades](gear-upgrades.md)).
