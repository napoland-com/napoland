Level 20 stays the top, but stashing keeps counting: past it, every 1,500 XP earns a merit, spent in the wardrobe at your chest on a look others see: one of six patterns on your jacket (over an outfit too) or one of six badges on your name tag, each for a merit, bought once and kept, and later glowcap mark shapes. Looks give no power at all. They sit in the same wardrobe as [outfits](outfits.md), in tabs of their own, and need sign-in to buy, as outfits do to wear.

Why: stashing stops meaning anything at level 20 today, and the players who play most lose the loop's reward.

More: [The loop](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-loop).
