The first region past the Near Woods (depth 2), beyond the end cabin: about 80 by 100, its heart about 150 steps in, draining twice as fast. Reaching it and coming back takes about level 8, sturdy gear and a fed fire on the way. Its own finds (2 or 3 new resources, new charms), its own surge and storm clocks, and its own paper map to find there. The parts that need a group wait for [group-mechanics](group-mechanics.md), and deeper regions after it for [deeper-regions](deeper-regions.md).

Why: the Near Woods' deepest tile is 25 seconds of walking from the edge, so a new player reaches everything on the first trip, and levels and gear have nowhere to take you. Solo players still progress, just more slowly, as the design says.

More: [Cooperation](../docs/DESIGN.md#cooperation-how-groups-go-farther) (solo players must still progress).
