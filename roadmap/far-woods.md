A trail climbs north from the cabin at the end of the Near Woods into **the Far Woods**, the first region at depth 2: about 80 by 100 tiles, its heart about 150 steps in, where you tire twice as fast. An old logging road and camp, one shelter whose fire burns down (the trapper's cabin), and a dark hollow where the rocks hum back and NAPO left a field post. Its own finds (cedar bark that burns, old batteries, a new charm, more shards and strange objects), its own surge and storm clocks set so they never overlap the Near Woods', and its map, found whole in the trapper's cabin. Reaching it and coming back takes about level 8, sturdy gear and a fed fire on the way. Its own rain comes with [regional-weather](regional-weather.md), which it does not wait for.

Why: the Near Woods' deepest tile is 25 seconds' walk from the edge, so a new player reaches everything on the first trip, and levels and gear have nowhere to take you. Solo players still progress, just more slowly.
