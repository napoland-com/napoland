The Burn's thread is what NAPO's crews wrote before the woods burned. Their notebooks lie in the ash, charred black and unreadable, until rain washes the ash off the page (a note that shows only in the rain, like Wren's crayon): then it says what the crew saw the night of the answer, from inside the light. Each page is one crew member's, in a field worker's plain shorthand, and together they say why the field post's relief never came.

Why: the Burn is about loss, and this lets it tell it slowly, in weather you wait for. The Long Dark.
