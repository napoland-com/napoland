The residents look after whoever comes back. The first time you sign in, a welcome parcel waits in your chest at home: resin, cloth, a thermos and road flares, enough to feel the difference on a first trip. After that, the first time you play each day (calendar days, UTC), signed in, the day's parcel goes into your chest, never into the bag. The parcels follow a calendar of seven days, Monday to Sunday, the same for everyone and pinned on the notice board. Play on all seven days of a week and Sunday's parcel also holds a **NAPO lockbox**, sealed since the evacuation, which you open at your chest: shards, a strange object, a charm, or a good stack of cloth and wire.

What comes in a parcel earns no stash XP: it was not brought home from out there. Missing a day costs only that week's lockbox; a new week starts fresh on Monday, and nothing is ever taken away. Guests see the calendar on the notice board, and that signing in brings the parcels (the Sign in button is under their name).

Why: a reason to come back each day that fits the story (nothing comes into the Zone from outside any more: the parcels are the town's own stores, shared out), and the lockbox gives the week a small goal without a mission (pillar 1: nothing is asked of you but coming back). Rested XP ([well-rested](well-rested.md)) rewards time away; this rewards coming back often; neither takes anything away.
