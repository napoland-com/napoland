title: The Long Night, once a week
status: idea

One night a week, at a fixed time on the wall clock, lasts three times as long, is always an aurora, and wire and strange objects grow twice as fast. The town's fires are not tended during it: everyone's fuel keeps the lodge's fire going, and if it goes out, next week's bonus is lost.

Why: a date the whole town prepares for, without a mission or a fight. Don't Starve Together's seasons. Changes a written decision (the fires in town never go out), so it is the owner's call; could lead into [seasons](seasons.md).
