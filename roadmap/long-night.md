Once a week, from Saturday 19:12 UTC (a dawn: game days start every 48 minutes from midnight UTC), a whole game day turns into one Long Night: 48 minutes of aurora instead of 16, copper wire and strange objects growing back twice as fast, and the watchers restless throughout. In the story it is the night of the week NAPO answered, and the woods answer longest. That night the lodge's fire is not tended: Walt asks for fuel, and everyone's resin and cloth keep it going. If it lasts until dawn, next week's Long Night keeps its bonus; if it goes out, next week's is only long and dark. The home fire, the old cabin's and the NAPO Bunker's stay tended, so a new player always has a safe fire.

Why: a date the whole town prepares for, without a mission or a fight: you bring fuel for a fire everyone shares. Don't Starve Together's seasons. It changes a written decision (the fires in town never go out) for the lodge's fire alone, on that night alone.
