A fire out in the wilds remembers the last 3 people who fed it, and an arrow already knows who painted it. When you warm at a fire someone else fed, or walk the way their arrow points, the text box offers you once to thank them. A thanks gives the helper 3 energy if they are out there right then (at most 5 times a trip) and a float, and when they come home they read a letter: "While you were away, 4 people thanked you for the fire at the ranger's hut." Each player can thank each helper once a day, and a new feat, Good neighbor, at 25 thanks, makes your arrows last two days. Who thanked whom is kept for 7 days, so the privacy page changes with it.

Why: fires and marks already let players help each other without meeting, but the helper never learns it did anything. Death Stranding's likes: a nudge, never XP.
