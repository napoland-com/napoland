A fire remembers the last 3 people who fed it; a mark remembers who painted it. Warming at someone's fire or following their arrow offers a one-time "Thank Ana" in the text box. Thanks earn the helper a little (+3 energy on the spot if they are out there, at most 5 a trip), and coming home they read a short letter: "While you were away, 4 people warmed at fires you fed; 2 followed your arrow by the pond." Each player can thank each helper once a day. A new feat, Good neighbor, at 25 thanks: your marks last two days.

Why: fires and marks already let players help each other without meeting, but the helper never learns it did anything. Death Stranding's likes. Keeping who helped whom is new data about players, so the privacy page changes with it.
