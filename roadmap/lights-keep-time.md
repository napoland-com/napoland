The cutters' board says the lights over the water are not lanterns and lead nowhere. They swell and fade the same for everyone, and they keep the Tower's 40-minute round. Stand where the long boardwalk ends and watch one round through: they go out in the order of the names in the cutters' book, one per cutter, and the last name is the one who is not in the book. A page of your field notes opens the first time you watch a whole round from there. No marker, no timer: you only learn that it is worth being there at the right minute, which is the same thing the Tower's clock has been telling the town all along.

Why: a reason to be somewhere at a certain time in a world that runs on a schedule you learn. Outer Wilds.
