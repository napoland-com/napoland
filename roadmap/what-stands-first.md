A player collapsed under one of NAPO's gates, came back for their pile and could never pull at the gate again: while anything lay on their own tile, A picked it up instead, and a full bag left the pile there for good. Now A uses what you face first, whoever you talk to or whatever stands there (a gate, a sign, a person, a fire), and only then picks up what lies on your tile or the one you face. What lies on the ground is still yours to take: face away from the gate and press A, or tap the pile. Getting someone up who is down still comes before everything.

Why: the things that move you on (a gate, a person with something to say) must never be blocked by what happens to lie on the ground. Together you go farther ([design](../docs/DESIGN.md#pillars)): a gate that takes two must answer every pull.
