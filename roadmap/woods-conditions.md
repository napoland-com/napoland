Every day (48 minutes) the Near Woods draws one or two conditions from a list of about a dozen in `content/`, announced at dawn by Mira and the notice board: the creek is up (the ford is closed, go round by the power line), thick fog (you see 5 tiles), a supply drop by the pond (3 crates of scrap and wire, first come first served), the watchers moved north, a shelter fire went out overnight. On top of that, a weekly one that everyone shares and talks about (copper week: wire by every pole; quiet woods: the watchers sleep). The board shows this week's and next week's, and they come round again, so nothing is ever missed for good. The map never changes, only what happens on it.

Why: today the tenth trip is the same as the first. Pacific Drive's junction conditions and Deep Rock Galactic's weekly deep dive make one fixed map worth learning again.
