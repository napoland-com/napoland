Every day (48 minutes) the Near Woods draws one or two conditions from a list of twelve in `content/`, starting at dawn: thick fog (you see about 5 tiles), a NAPO cache the creek washed out of its bank by the pond (scrap and wire, first come first served), the watchers moved north (or came closer), a shelter fire went out overnight, glowcaps everywhere, the firs weeping resin, flares or scrap by the old car, and more. A banner says so at dawn, Mira mentions them and the notice board lists them. On top of that, a weekly one that everyone shares and talks about (copper week: wire by every pole; quiet woods: the watchers sleep). The board shows this week's and next week's, and they come round again in a fixed order, so nothing is ever missed for good. The map never changes, only what happens on it.

Why: today the tenth trip is the same as the first. Pacific Drive's junction conditions and Deep Rock Galactic's weekly deep dive make one fixed map worth learning again.
