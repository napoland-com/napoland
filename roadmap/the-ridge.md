The region at depth 4, above the Burn: up the trappers' fixed rope north of the scar, which holds only with three on it at once, the land climbs out of the firs into snow. It is always winter up there, you tire four times as fast as in the Near Woods, and it has one shelter, the trappers' high hut, whose fire nobody keeps.

What is new up there:

- **Three on the rope.** The next step after NAPO's two-person gate into the Burn: the rope takes three pulling within 5 seconds, one to a tile, and all three go up together. The way down needs nobody.
- **Footprints in the snow.** Every step anyone takes on the Ridge stays in the snow for an hour, for everyone to see and follow, never whose. Whoever comes up after you can follow your way to the hut or the crest, or home.
- **Whiteout.** It snows once a day, and while it does you see 3 tiles and no more: the tracks are the way.
- **The icefall and crampons.** A slope of old ice from the high hut straight up to the snowfield, climbed only in crampons, made at the workbench from rime quartz found only on the Ridge's crest: the first trip goes the long way up the switchbacks, for the quartz.

Why: farther is harder and richer, and together you go farther: three people, not two, and the footprints make even strangers who were never online at the same time walk together. It fits the story too: the trappers worked this line in threes, and their cairn on the crest says the needles spin past it, with the snow glowing at night from underneath, farther in than NAPO ever went.
