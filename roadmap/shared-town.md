The first playable napoland: several players walk around Stonebrook together, in real time, in the browser on phones and desktops. The server owns every step and the client predicts your own, so walking feels instant and nobody can walk faster than the rules allow. Players are saved in a database. The look (dark low-poly, in the mood of Pacific Drive) and the controls (a joystick, A and B, no map) come from here.

Why: everything else is built on a shared world that feels good to walk in on a phone.

More: [Movement and controls](../docs/DESIGN.md#movement-and-controls), [how movement works](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#movement-authoritative-server-predicted-client).
