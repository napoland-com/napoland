On a computer, **WASD** and the **arrow keys** walk: tile by tile, one of four directions, with the joystick's rules (a quick tap on a new direction turns you in place, holding walks, the direction you already face walks at once). **E** or **Space** is A (pick up, talk, open), **Q** or **Escape** is B (bag, back), and **Enter** opens the chat, as in Metin2 (Enter on an empty line closes it again). The mouse keeps working as a finger, and nothing ever needs the keyboard: the game stays mobile-first.

Why: people playing on a computer move more comfortably with keys, and it makes play-testing faster for everyone working on the game.
