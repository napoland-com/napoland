Someone opening napoland for the first time plays at once: they choose a name and walk out of the door, as a guest. A guest character lives in that browser, like the characters from before sign-in. Top left, under the name, the status panel says it is a guest session and shows a **Sign in** button: a guest's progress is lost with the browser's data, and a guest who stays away for 30 days is deleted. Signing in keeps everything, because the guest character becomes the account's (the claim that already works for characters made before sign-in).

Signing in also opens what needs a real person behind it: chat, friends and trading (for safety, as today), the daily parcels ([daily-parcels](daily-parcels.md)) and outfits ([outfits](outfits.md)). The card that asks for sign-in says so in one line.

Why: a first visit should end in the woods, not at an email form. Players who come back sign in to keep what they built, which is the moment it is worth it to them. It changes a written decision (sign-in first), so DESIGN.md changes with it, and the privacy policy says what a guest session keeps and for how long.
