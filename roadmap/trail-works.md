Two fixed places in the Near Woods stand broken until the whole server brings the materials: a footbridge over the creek by the pond (30 scrap), which opens a 14-step shortcut home from the far bank, and a street light in the pond clearing (12 copper wire), which keeps off surges and hitchhikers like any street light. Materials are given at the place (it asks first: "Give 3 scrap to the footbridge?"), progress shows there and on the notice board, and a plaque names who gave the most. What is built wears (the bridge needs 5 scrap a day, the light 2 copper wire, from what was given ahead, a week of it at most), so it is kept up, never finished; when it runs out, it stands broken again. The map never changes shape: the place is always there, whole or broken. The state is the whole server's, kept across restarts.

Why: spare materials get a use, the world changes because of players, and a small player base shares one goal, like the Old Stone. Death Stranding's roads and bridges.

Later: a rope down a ledge, and a rain lean-to.
