Two fixed places in the Near Woods stand broken until the whole server brings the materials: a footbridge over the creek by the pond, which opens a 14-step shortcut, and a street light in the pond clearing, which keeps off surges and hitchhikers like any street light. Progress shows at the place and on the notice board, and a plaque names who gave the most. What is built wears (the bridge needs 5 scrap a day, the light 2 copper wire), so it is kept up, never finished. The map never changes shape: the place is always there, whole or broken. A rope down a ledge and a rain lean-to come later.

Why: spare materials get a use, the world changes because of players, and a small player base shares one goal, like the Old Stone. Death Stranding's roads and bridges.
