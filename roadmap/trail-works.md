Fixed places in the wilds stand broken until the whole server brings the materials (A, facing them): a footbridge over the stream that opens a shortcut (30 scrap, 20 wire), a street light at the pond clearing that keeps off surges and hitchhikers (10 wire, 2 shards), a rope down a ledge, a rain lean-to. Progress shows at the place and on the notice board, and a plaque names who gave. What is built wears slowly (a bridge needs 5 scrap a day), so it is kept up, never finished. The map stays fixed: the place is always there, whole or broken.

Why: surplus materials get a use, the world changes because of players, and a small player base shares one goal, like the Old Stone. Death Stranding's roads and bridges.
