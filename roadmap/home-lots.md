Your home stands in a garden of its own instead of on a street: a clearing in the forest, a picket fence all round it, the house at the back. Nobody walks in or out: the only way to town is NAPO's teleport inside the house, and the one by the notice board brings you back. Friends visit from the friends list: Visit on a friend's card sets you down by the teleport in their home, whether they are in or not, to look round the house they made theirs and walk out into their garden; a setting under Friends keeps visitors out.

The house starts as a garage, and the workbench builds it up with what your stash holds: a log cabin, then a house with two floors and a porch. Each level changes how it looks, outside and in, and opens something new inside: the cabin a kitchen that cooks from your chest, the house a map table that shows how things stand out there, as the notice board in town does.

Why: a home that is yours alone, and a house to build up over many trips, is something to come back to and something to show your friends (leave home, come back; together you go farther). Building it up asks for what only farther regions give, so it pulls you out farther (farther is harder and richer). A street was a place to get lost on: new players walked out of the wrong door, and nothing but a plate told one cabin from another.

Replaces the street of [streets](streets.md) and the road of [street-visits](street-visits.md).
