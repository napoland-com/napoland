Out on the road, the bag shows what you wear, piece by piece, with how worn each one is, so you can check your gear anywhere, not only at the chest. Gear can now travel in the bag: a piece you carry takes a slot and weighs what it weighs, and you can put it on right where you stand, with what it replaces going into the bag if there is room. Gear that comes your way away from home (the piece a strange object turns out to be when you look at it in town, a friend's trade) can be worn at once.

Each carried piece keeps its own condition, quirk and upgrade, as it does in the chest. If you collapse, what you wear stays on you, as today, and a piece in the bag falls into your pile like anything else you carry. The bag you wear is the one thing that only changes at home.

As built: at the chest a piece in the stash has Take it out beside Wear, and comes out as it is, one piece to a bag slot. The bag shows a Wearing row under what you carry, and every slot and piece in it opens a card, as in the chest: a worn piece's button takes it off into the bag ("Take off (it goes into your bag)", greyed out with a word when the bag is full), a carried piece's puts it on ("Wear (your worn cap goes into your bag)", or "Wear"), and what it replaces takes its slot; A and a double tap do the same, B backs out a step, and Throw away sits beside it and asks first. Putting on and taking off ask nothing. The bag you wear says it changes only at home, and a carried bag goes on only from the stash. A carried piece falls into your pile as it is, and comes back out of it so, for you or in someone else's half; a watcher may take one. What a strange object turns into is a piece in the bag at once, its quirk rolled, and the text box names the quirk. What you wear counts as taken out of the stash, so a piece earns its XP once however it comes home.

Why: choosing gear stays part of planning a trip, since spare gear costs a slot and weight, but a trip can now answer what happens on it. It changes a written decision (gear only at home), so DESIGN.md changes with it.
