A permanent tool in your bag: an old paper map of a region, drawn by hand, with its roads, trails, cabins, water and a few landmarks. It **never shows where you are**. You work that out yourself by matching what you see around you (houses, signs, poles, the shape of a trail, a pond) to the drawing. Tools are never used up and stay with you when you collapse, like equipment. The bag's header has one map button (M on a keyboard): it opens the map of the area you are in, indoors the area outside. For now every area has one (Stonebrook, the Near Woods, the South Road), and everyone carries them all; maps take no slot and weigh nothing. Tap the paper to see it full size, B to put it away. Farther out there will be areas without a map, until you find it. A tool is an item of its own kind, and each player's tools are their own, saved with them since [owned-tools](owned-tools.md): everyone starts with the same three maps, and the first map you can earn joins the map button.

Why: napoland stays mapless in spirit (no minimap, no marker, no GPS), but an old map rewards learning the land and makes getting lost part of the journey, as in The Long Dark.
