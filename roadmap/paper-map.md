A permanent tool in your bag: an old paper map of a region, drawn by hand, with its roads, trails, cabins, water and a few landmarks. It **never shows where you are**. You work that out yourself by matching what you see around you (houses, signs, poles, the shape of a trail, a pond) to the drawing. Tools are never used up and stay with you when you collapse, like equipment. Everyone carries the map of the Near Woods; maps of farther regions come later, found or earned.

Why: napoland stays mapless in spirit (no minimap, no marker, no GPS), but an old map rewards learning the land and makes getting lost part of the journey, as in The Long Dark.
