A permanent tool in your bag: an old paper map of a region, drawn by hand, with its roads, trails, cabins, water and a few landmarks. It **never shows where you are**. You work that out yourself by matching what you see around you (houses, signs, poles, the shape of a trail, a pond) to the drawing. Tools are never used up and stay with you when you collapse, like equipment. Everyone carries the map of the Near Woods, in the bag's header: it takes no slot and weighs nothing. Tap it for the drawing, tap the paper to see it full size, B to put it away. Maps of farther regions come later, found or earned. A tool is already an item of its own kind; the first map you can earn also saves each player's tools (today everyone has the same one, so nothing is saved).

Why: napoland stays mapless in spirit (no minimap, no marker, no GPS), but an old map rewards learning the land and makes getting lost part of the journey, as in The Long Dark.
