The world itself wears you down, like in Pacific Drive. Five elements (heat, cold, wind, electricity, radiation) each have their own resistance. Day and night, and weather per region, change what you find and how dangerous it is. Anomalies are events that everyone on the map shares, announced before they hit: a storm rolls over a region, a flash makes a patch of ground glow and then discharge, a surge breaks out near the Old Stone. Being caught costs energy, less with the right resistance; a shelter protects from everything; some anomalies leave rare finds behind. Hand warmers and rad tablets give resistance for a while. The server owns the sky, so everyone sees the same one.

Already live ([survival-dynamics](survival-dynamics.md)): the day with aurora nights, and the Near Woods' surge clock. Still to come: the five elements and their resistances, storms and flashes, weather per region.

Why: tension comes from the world, never from fights.
