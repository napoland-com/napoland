The world itself wears you down, like in Pacific Drive. Five elements (heat, cold, wind, electricity, radiation) each have their own resistance, and now each has something to resist. Anomalies are events that everyone on the map shares, announced before they hit: a **storm** rolls over the Near Woods on its own clock, a minute's warning before it (wind and electricity, it soaks you, being wet in its wind chills you, less to see, shards by the old poles while it blows); a **flash** makes a patch of ground near someone glow and then discharge (a spark is electric, a fire flash hot); a **surge** breaks out near the Old Stone's shards. Being caught costs energy, less with the right resistance; a roof protects from everything. The status panel says what drains you, element by element. The server owns all of it, so everyone sees the same sky.

Still to come ([regional-weather](regional-weather.md)): each region with its own weather cycle, and hand warmers and rad tablets.

Why: tension comes from the world, never from fights, and gear only matters if the world asks for it.
