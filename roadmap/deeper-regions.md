New regions beyond the Near Woods, to the north, each deeper than the last, each with its own climate (rainforest, snowy ridge, marsh, burnt forest...), weather and finds. The deeper, the harder and the richer, and the deepest places need a group. The map stays fixed, so players can share routes and guides, and it grows as regions are added farther out. The world gets deeper toward the stones that hum back, so that is where they open; the South Road, the other way, ends at the quarantine line ([the story](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-story-napo-and-napoland)).

The first is built: [the Far Woods](far-woods.md), depth 2, up the trappers' trail from the cabin at the end of the Near Woods, where one player at about level 8 can go and come back. Its map is the first that is found out there, not given: in the trapper's cabin. **Built:** the second, [the Burn](../docs/DESIGN.md#world-structure), depth 3, north of the Far Woods' hollow (NAPO's field post log says the needles would not settle north of it), behind NAPO's gate, which opens only for two pulling at once and takes them both through (the first of [group-mechanics](group-mechanics.md)' group gates); the way back needs nobody. A forest the answer burned: black snags over ash, no rain and no storm but a short surge and many flashes, quicker creatures, the line cabin (its one fire), the scar at its heart, more shards and strange objects than the Far Woods, and fused glass, found only there. Its map lies whole in the line cabin for now; maps found in pieces come with [inked-maps](inked-maps.md). Regions after it, depth 4 and on, are items of their own, north of the Burn.

Why: farther is harder and richer, and one fixed world is one you can learn.
