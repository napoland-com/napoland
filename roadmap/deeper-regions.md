New regions beyond the Near Woods, to the north, each deeper than the last, each with its own climate (rainforest, snowy ridge, marsh, burnt forest...), weather and finds. The deeper, the harder and the richer, and the deepest places need a group. The map stays fixed, so players can share routes and guides, and it grows as regions are added farther out. The world gets deeper toward the stones that hum back, so that is where they open; the South Road, the other way, ends at the quarantine line ([the story](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-story-napo-and-napoland)). Every area so far has its paper map, and the map shows the area you are in; deeper regions will have none until their maps are found out there.

Why: farther is harder and richer, and one fixed world is one you can learn.

More: [World structure](../docs/DESIGN.md#world-structure), [Pillars](../docs/DESIGN.md#pillars).
