New regions beyond the Near Woods, each deeper than the last, each with its own climate (rainforest, snowy ridge, marsh, burnt forest...), weather and finds. The deeper, the harder and the richer, and the deepest places need a group. The map stays fixed, so players can share routes and guides, and it grows as regions are added farther out.

Why: farther is harder and richer, and one fixed world is one you can learn.

More: [World structure](../docs/DESIGN.md#world-structure), [Pillars](../docs/DESIGN.md#pillars).
