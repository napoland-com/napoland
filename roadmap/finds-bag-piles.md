Things to pick up in the Near Woods and in the rooms (glowcaps, fir resin, scrap metal, copper wire, cloth scraps, anomaly shards, a thermos that gives back energy) and a bag with 8 slots to carry them. Finds are shared: when someone takes one, a new one grows later somewhere else in the same kind of place, and deep finds stay deep. If you collapse, what you carried falls where you fell: you get all of it back, anyone else who finds it first gets a random half, and the pile fades after an hour.

Why: a reason to go farther, and something to lose when you push your luck.
