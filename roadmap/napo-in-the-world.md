Napoland's story shows in the world itself: old NAPO facilities, signs, antennas, laboratories, shelters, warning signs and abandoned equipment turn up throughout the map.

Already live ([south-road](south-road.md), [richer-places](richer-places.md), [far-woods](far-woods.md)): NAPO's old grounds down Stonebrook's south road, with the bunker, the research station and its laboratory, the motor pool across the road (its box trucks and fuel pump), the Tower, the field site with its sample cages (rocks from deep in the woods behind steel mesh, each with its tag) and the checkpoint, and the people who stayed. In the Near Woods, NAPO's Zone warning where the road comes in, one of its field jeeps burned out off the road across from the last light, its survey stakes at the foot of the rocks, and its listening post (a mast and a notice) by the ring of stones, the rocks that hum back; in Stonebrook, its evacuation notice by the south road. In the Far Woods, its field post in the dark hollow where the rocks hum back loudest: a small concrete hut, dark and cold, with the post's log on its desk (NAPO's last word from up there, a chapter of the story), its mast snapped halfway and its light dead, its warning sign where the trail comes through the rim, and its field kit's old batteries in the crates and around the hut.

Still to come: more of NAPO's places as the [deeper regions](deeper-regions.md) open north of the Far Woods (a field post wherever the rocks hum back), and NAPO's equipment carried home: its first-aid stores as consumables (rad tablets, hand warmers: [regional-weather](regional-weather.md)) and a use at the workbench for the old batteries its field kit ran on. What stands in the world is placed by the map generators, so it stays fixed. None of it is a mission: the story comes only from what you see, read and hear out there.

Why: the world already feels left behind (most of Stonebrook left after the lights started showing up in the woods; scrap comes from cars and cabins nobody came back for), and NAPO says why. What it left makes landmarks to learn the land by and to name in routes and guides (one fixed world you learn), and it tells the story without a single mission (no fights, no PvP, no missions).
