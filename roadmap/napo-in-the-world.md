Napoland's story shows in the world itself: old NAPO facilities, signs, antennas, laboratories, shelters, warning signs and abandoned equipment turn up throughout the map. It starts in the maps there are now, for example with warning signs on the way into the Near Woods, an antenna, equipment left where it was dropped. The bigger places come as the [deeper regions](deeper-regions.md) open, among them a NAPO Research Station, a NAPO Tower, a NAPO Laboratory and a NAPO Bunker. Some of the abandoned equipment can be carried home, as finds or gear. What stands in the world is placed by the map generators, so it stays fixed. None of it is a mission: the story comes only from what you see, read and hear out there.

Why: the world already feels left behind (most of Stonebrook left after the lights started showing up in the woods; scrap comes from cars and cabins nobody came back for), and NAPO says why. What it left makes landmarks to learn the land by and to name in routes and guides (one fixed world you learn), and it tells the story without a single mission (no fights, no PvP, no missions).
