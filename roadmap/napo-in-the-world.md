Napoland's story shows in the world itself: old NAPO facilities, signs, antennas, laboratories, shelters, warning signs and abandoned equipment turn up throughout the map.

Already live ([south-road](south-road.md)): NAPO's old grounds down Stonebrook's south road, with the bunker, the research station and its laboratory, the Tower, the field site and the checkpoint, and the people who stayed.

Still to come: NAPO's traces in the Near Woods, for example warning signs on the way in, an antenna, equipment left where it was dropped; more of NAPO's places as the [deeper regions](deeper-regions.md) open; and some of the abandoned equipment carried home, as finds or gear. What stands in the world is placed by the map generators, so it stays fixed. None of it is a mission: the story comes only from what you see, read and hear out there.

Why: the world already feels left behind (most of Stonebrook left after the lights started showing up in the woods; scrap comes from cars and cabins nobody came back for), and NAPO says why. What it left makes landmarks to learn the land by and to name in routes and guides (one fixed world you learn), and it tells the story without a single mission (no fights, no PvP, no missions).
