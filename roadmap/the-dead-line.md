Why did the street lights come on with no power? Numbered tags on the poles; a lineman's notes that list pole numbers; and in each player's notebook one of 24 torn pieces of a wiring diagram, so nobody can finish it alone and the pieces have to be put together in chat. The diagram gives an order to face the poles in; done on an aurora night at the lamp with no wires, it opens something new. Each later region adds a layer.

Why: something for the whole community to work on together, and a long-term why. Animal Well's bunny mural, Tunic's manual.
