Why did the lamp with no wires come on, the night of the answer? Every pole on the old north line carries a tin tag with a number (1 to 6 in Stonebrook, 7 to 16 in the Near Woods; the South Road's poles are NAPO's line and carry none). Walt's notes say which way some of them lean, and each player's journal holds one of four torn pieces of NAPO's wiring diagram, so the pieces have to be put together in chat. On an aurora night, facing the poles' leanings in the right order at the lamp puts it out for everyone until dawn, opens a field page for every player in the woods, and Walt answers it in one of his scenes. A server restart relights it.

Why: something for the whole community to work on together, and a long-term why. Animal Well's bunny mural, Tunic's manual.
