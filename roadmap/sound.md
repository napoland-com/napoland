The game is silent. Rain, wind in the firs, a fire that crackles quieter as it burns low, footsteps that change on road, grass, mud and floors, the power lines humming on aurora nights. Warnings you hear before you read them: a low tone as the region grows restless, the surge coming closer from deep in, thunder a moment after the lightning, a faint sound while a watcher moves and silence when it freezes. Sounds are made in the browser or bundled as small files (nothing loaded from other sites, no large binaries). Volume and mute in the menu.

Why: half of the mood and every warning today live in banners. In the dark, sound is how you read danger you cannot see (Darkwood, The Long Dark).
