At 0 energy out in the wilds you slump for 90 seconds (180 with a flare burning by you) before you collapse. Everyone on the map hears it in local chat by landmark, never by position ("Ana is down by the pond"): the landmarks are the maps' named places, which [richer-places](richer-places.md) fills out. Anyone who reaches you, faces you and presses A gives you 20 of their own energy, and you get up. Both earn a thanks.

Why: the rescue idea in [group-mechanics](group-mechanics.md) needs a friend online and nearby; this makes any stranger on the map worth something, and gives players a moment they retell. Don't Starve Together's Telltale Heart, which costs the rescuer.
