At 0 energy you slump for 90 seconds before you collapse (twice as long with a flare lit). Everyone on the map hears it in local chat by landmark ("Ana is down by the pond"), never by position. Anyone who reaches you and presses A gives you 20 of their own energy and you get up. Both earn a thanks.

Why: the rescue idea in [group-mechanics](group-mechanics.md) needs a friend online and nearby; this makes any stranger on the map worth something, and gives players a moment they retell. Don't Starve Together's Telltale Heart, which costs the rescuer health.

More: [Cooperation](../docs/DESIGN.md#cooperation-how-groups-go-farther).
