At 0 energy out in the wilds you slump where you stand for 90 seconds (180 with a flare burning by you) before you collapse: you cannot walk or act, your figure lies on the ground for everyone to see, and your screen shows a quiet countdown and "Someone may come." Everyone on the map hears it once in local chat by landmark, never by position ("Ana is down by the pond, 9 steps from the ranger's hut", "Ana is down 12 steps from the power line"): the same rule, in the shared code, as the [trip report](trip-report.md)'s where you fell, counted in walking steps to the nearest door, place the map names ([richer-places](richer-places.md) fills them out), way on, power line, wreck, sign, road or way home. Anyone who reaches you, faces you and presses A is asked to give you 20 of their own energy (it takes more than 20 to give), and you get up with it. Getting up thanks whoever came, as any thanks does: it counts for their Good neighbor, and their letter home says "for getting Ana back up by the pond, 9 steps from the ranger's hut". Nothing touches you while you are down: no creature, and no surge, storm or flash drains you further. If nobody comes, you collapse as before.

Why: the rescue idea in [group-mechanics](group-mechanics.md) needs a friend online and nearby; this makes any stranger on the map worth something, and gives players a moment they retell. Don't Starve Together's Telltale Heart, which costs the rescuer.
