Four rooms a long way out have one of NAPO's teleports, set up as an outpost: the dormitory on the South Road, the NAPO field post in the Far Woods, the cutters' hut in the Marsh and the high hut on the Ridge. A at it asks first ("Go home? It sets you down inside your own home."), then sets you down in front of the one in your own house, and the trip ends as walking in does. It goes one way: there is none at home to come back by, so the walk out again is the price, and the way there stays as far as it was.

Why: a trip that goes deep can end where it is safe instead of turning every step back, and a hut or barracks becomes a place you aim for. Resident Evil's safe rooms, kept to the pillars (no fights, leave home and come back).
