New things to forage with rules like today's finds: huckleberries in clearings, fiddleheads by the stream, chanterelles only after rain. Cook them at any burning fire into meals that last one trip, two at a time and never two of the same: fir-tip tea (15 more energy on the bar for 12 minutes), chanterelle stew (15% cold resistance), berry pemmican (the bag feels 15% lighter). Eating at home before you leave is part of planning a trip, like gear.

Why: a trip has little to prepare besides gear, fires only take, and rain only hurts (here it grows chanterelles). Valheim's food, The Long Dark's cooking.
