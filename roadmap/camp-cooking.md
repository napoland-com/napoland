New things to forage in the Near Woods, with the same rules as today's finds: huckleberries in the clearings, fiddleheads by the stream, fir tips at the forest's edge, and chanterelles that come up only after the rain. Cook them at any burning fire (at home, the lodge's, the NAPO Bunker's, or a shelter's someone kept fed) into meals that last one trip, two at a time and never two of the same: fir-tip tea (15 more energy on the bar), chanterelle stew (cold bites 15% less), berry pemmican (the bag feels 15% lighter). Eating at home before you leave is part of planning a trip, like choosing gear.

Why: a trip has little to prepare besides gear, fires only take, and rain only hurts (here it grows chanterelles). Valheim's food, The Long Dark's cooking.
