New things to forage in the Near Woods, with the same rules as today's finds: huckleberries in the clearings, fiddleheads by the creek, fir tips at the forest's edge, and chanterelles that come up only after the rain. Cook them at any burning fire (at home, the lodge's, the NAPO Bunker's, or a shelter's someone kept fed) into meals that last one trip, two at a time and never two of the same: fir-tip tea (15 more energy on the bar), chanterelle stew (cold bites 15% less), berry pemmican (the bag feels 15% lighter). Eating at home before you leave is part of planning a trip, like choosing gear.

Built: fir tips (3 make a tea), huckleberries (5 make pemmican), fiddleheads and chanterelles (2 and 3 make a stew) grow like any find; chanterelles only while it rains over the Near Woods, by their own rain and the season's, and for 8 minutes after. A at a fire that burns offers "Feed the fire" or "Cook" when you carry something to cook, and cooking asks first with what it uses. A meal waits in the bag until it is eaten, which asks first too; it works until you walk into your cabin or collapse, and the status panel says what you ate and what it does. The field notes have a page for the food, one for chanterelles (whose question fills in when you pick one in the rain) and one for meals.

Why: a trip has little to prepare besides gear, fires only take, and rain only hurts (here it grows chanterelles). Valheim's food, The Long Dark's cooking.
