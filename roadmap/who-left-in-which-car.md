The South Road's thread is the people who left. Four cars of the jam each carry a luggage tag that names a family and its street in Stonebrook, and each dark house in town (the Okadas', the Hales', the Dahls', the Lindqvists') has its family name on the mailbox: read a tag, then walk to the mailbox, and you know which family drove away in it, and what it still holds. The Dahls' car is the one with the child seat and the open door, third along the line. Reading its tag is what opens Edith's scene "The car", once she is home: she learns Kari's car got as far as the jam. Nothing is marked: you match a tag to a name by walking between the two places.

**Built:** the four tags are notes by "The people who left" (`leavers`), laid by the South Road's generator on four jam cars, so they count as notes: a first read and the journal's author heading. They say the family, the street and what rode in the car; none says where they went.

Why: it ties the South Road back to Stonebrook the way the north line ties the Near Woods to it, and answers "where did everyone go" with what stands there, never with a mission. Firewatch, What Remains of Edith Finch.
