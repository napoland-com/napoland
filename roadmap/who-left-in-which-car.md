The South Road's thread is the people who left. Each car in the jam has a plate and a luggage tag that names a street of Stonebrook, and each dark house in town (the Okadas', the Hales', the Dahls', the Lindqvists') has its family name on the mailbox: read a car, then read the mailbox, and a page of your field notes says which family drove away in it, and what it still holds. The Dahls' car is the one with the child seat and the door left open, and finding it is what finishes Kari's page for Edith ("Kari", her scene). Nothing is marked: you match a plate to a name by walking between the two places.

Why: it ties the South Road back to Stonebrook the way the north line ties the Near Woods to it, and answers "where did everyone go" with what stands there, never with a mission. Firewatch, What Remains of Edith Finch.
