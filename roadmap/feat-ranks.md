Each feat gets 5 ranks (Rain walker: rain soaks you 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50% slower at 1.5k, 5k, 15k, 40k and 100k steps in the rain). New feats: Mender (gear wears 5% slower a rank), Forager (a chance of a double find), Pathfinder (steps taken 95 or more from home). The status panel shows how far the next rank is.

Why: all four feats come in the first hours today; this is months of progress that comes from playing, not grinding. The Long Dark's skills.
