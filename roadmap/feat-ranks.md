Each feat gets 5 ranks, and rank 1 is today's feat, so nobody loses what they earned: Rain walker makes rain soak you 20, 30, 40, 45 and 50% slower at 1,500, 5,000, 15,000, 40,000 and 100,000 steps in the rain, and the other feats climb the same way. New feats: Mender (gear wears 5% slower a rank), Forager (a chance that a find comes up double) and Pathfinder (steps taken 85 or more from home, where the drain gets 3% gentler a rank). The status panel shows each feat's rank and how far the next one is, and a new rank gets a banner.

Why: all four feats come in the first hours today, then nothing. Ranks are months of progress that come from playing, never from grinding. The Long Dark's skills.
