A second button in the bag's header, next to the map button, opens the journal at its field notes: the journal is the one book the game keeps, so the notebook is a part of it, never a second book. It gets a page the first time you pick up each kind of find, read a sign or one of NAPO's desks, or live through something: a surge while under a light or a roof, a flash, a storm, an aurora, a hitchhiker, a watcher. Pages about the strange start with a blank ("It stops when... ?") that fills in only when you see it happen, so you learn the rules instead of being told them. Each region shows a count of its field pages ("The Near Woods: 23 of 41"), never of the story's chapters, and it never says where anything is.

Why: exploring leaves no record today, and a player on day 7 has nothing left to want. The notebook grows by itself as content is added, the new signs and landmarks of [richer-places](richer-places.md) too. Subnautica's databank, Pacific Drive's logbook, Outer Wilds' ship log.
