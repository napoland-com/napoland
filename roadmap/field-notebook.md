A notebook in the bag's header, next to the paper map (no slot, no weight). It gets a page the first time you pick up each kind of find, read a sign or a note, lie low through a surge, see a flash, a storm, an aurora, a hitchhiker or a watcher. Phenomena start with blanks ("It stops when... ?") that fill in only when you see it happen (a watcher freezing while someone faces it), so you learn the rules instead of being told them. Each region shows a count ("Near Woods: 23 of 41"). It never says where anything is, only that it exists.

Why: exploring leaves no record today, and day 7 has nothing to want. The notebook grows by itself as content is added. Subnautica's databank, Pacific Drive's logbook, Outer Wilds' ship log.
