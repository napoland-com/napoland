Stepping into your home after a trip shows a small card: minutes out, steps, the deepest place you reached (by name, never a spot), the XP you brought home, the lowest your energy got, and what you were caught in ("a storm, 2 flashes"). It keeps your bests (deepest, longest out, most XP in one trip). After a collapse it says where you fell, by landmark ("by the pond", "near the ranger's hut").

Why: every trip ends with a result and a story in one line, which players share in chat. Borrowed from the end-of-raid screens of extraction games.
