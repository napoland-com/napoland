Walking into your home after a trip shows a small card: how many minutes you were out, how many steps you took, the deepest place you reached (by its region and how far out, never a spot), what your bag will earn when you stash it, the lowest your energy got, and what you were caught in ("a storm, 2 flashes"). It keeps your bests (the farthest, the longest out, the most XP in one trip) and says when one is beaten. After a collapse it comes when you wake up, and says where you fell by a landmark ("by the pond, 12 steps from the crossroads"), from the maps' named places.

Why: every trip ends with a result and a story in one line, which players share in chat. Borrowed from the end-of-raid screens of extraction games.
