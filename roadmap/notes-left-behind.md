The whole world holds about 13 lines of story today. About 30 handwritten notes sit in fixed places (the ranger's hut, the end cabin, glove boxes, under the poles), left by the people who went away or never came back: the ranger, a lineman, the family in the end cabin. Every player can read each one once (5 XP the first time); the notebook keeps them. Some can only be read at night, in the rain or on an aurora night. The deepest say the most. Alongside them, one-of-a-kind keepsakes (a photo, a badge, a child's drawing) to take home once each; a full set gives a small lasting perk, like a feat.

Why: the only thing to find today is materials, and empty rooms have no reason to be entered. The signs already hint at a story ("No wires run to this light"); this tells it. Firewatch, Subnautica, The Long Dark.
