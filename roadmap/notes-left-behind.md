About 30 handwritten notes lie in fixed places: on the tables and shelves of the shelters, in the rusted car's glove box, nailed under the poles, in the empty house in town, and some on the South Road, where the leavers stopped. The ranger, Walt (from when he walked the line) and the Barlow family in the cabin at the end wrote them. Face one and press A to read it; the first read gives 5 XP, and the journal keeps it to read again at home. Some can only be read at night, in the rain or on an aurora night, and the deeper a note, the more it says. Keepsakes come after: one-of-a-kind things to take home once each, a full set giving a small lasting perk.

Why: the only thing to find out there is materials, and the rooms give no reason to go in. The signs already hint at a story; the notes tell it, one place at a time. Firewatch, Subnautica, The Long Dark.
