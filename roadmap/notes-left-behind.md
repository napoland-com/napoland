Thirty handwritten notes lie in fixed places: on the tables, shelves and beds of the shelters, in the old car where the north road gives out, nailed to the poles, in the empty house in town, and on the South Road, where the leavers stopped. The ranger, Walt (from when he walked the line) and the Barlow family from the cabin at the end wrote them, ten each. Face one and press A to read it; the first read gives 5 XP, and the journal's third part, Notes, keeps it by who wrote it, to read again at home. Some can only be read at night, in the rain or on an aurora night, and the deeper a note, the more it says. Five keepsakes come with them: one-of-a-kind things each player finds for themselves and takes home once, the whole set making the energy bar 5 bigger for good.

Why: the only thing to find out there was materials, and most rooms gave no reason to go in (NAPO's move the story on; the cabins and houses did not). The signs already hinted at a story; the notes tell it, one place at a time. Firewatch, Subnautica, The Long Dark.
