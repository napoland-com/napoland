A region at depth 1 for one player, up the brook from Stonebrook's millpond: the lake Stonebrook Timber Co. made to drive its mill, behind a dam across the valley's mouth. Calm like the South Road (the surges come down the woods and die at town): no surges, storms, flashes or creatures. Its danger is the water.

What is new there:

- **A lake on a clock.** Twice for every pulse of the Tower, starting as the Near Woods' surge breaks, most of the lake draws back for five minutes and its bed can be walked: the drowned valley of the first farms, the farm fallen in, the drowned lane and the farm's car, the stumps of the forest cut before the water came. Whoever is still out there when the water comes home is carried ashore, soaked. The water by the dam never draws back.
- **The Sister.** At the deepest of the drowned valley stands the Old Stone's twin, the standing stone of the first farms' meadow, read only at low water. Agnes's log says she hums before the Tower pulses, not after; what that means is left open.
- **Two who won't cross.** Agnes Brandt keeps the dam and its fire (the region's safe fire, in the keeper's house); Jon, her husband, opened the sluice for NAPO the week before the answer and has camped on a knoll in the lake since, reached only across the bed at low water (at high water he rows you off it into the boathouse). Her keeper's log and his chalk slates each tell one side, so the player carries the words, and each has a scene once the other's side is read.

Why: a calm place near town for a new player that still asks for timing (pillar 2: one more look, or back before the water), and a thread of its own told only by what stands there and what two people say. One fixed clock everyone learns.
