Gear wears down while you are out in the wilds; worn out, it stops protecting you until it is mended at the workbench with scrap and cloth. And the rarest gear (anomalous, found deep in) comes with a quirk of its own: boots that leave glowing footprints, a cap that makes street lights flicker as you pass, a coat that hums before a surge. Each piece has to be told apart from another of the same kind (how worn it is, which quirk it has), so gear becomes a thing of its own rather than a count in the stash.

Why: the other half of Pacific Drive's car and The Long Dark's clothing: what keeps you alive needs looking after, and a lucky find is unlike any other. Scrap and cloth keep a use after you have crafted your set.
