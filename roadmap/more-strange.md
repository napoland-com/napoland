Looking at a strange object in town shows one of 15 things instead of 7: two new charms (an ember coal, and fires warm you 20% faster; a pale moth, and crushing a glowcap gives you 1 energy), rare anomalous gear for every slot, which can be the kit of NAPO's crews in white suits, and now and then a handful of cloth. The rare gear together is 1 strange object in 20; charms stay about 1 in 4. Revealed gear lands in the bag, as it does today, and can be worn at once ([gear-on-the-road](gear-on-the-road.md)). Three new quirks come with it.

Built: fifteen outcomes by weight: 2 shards (a third), a thermos or 2 road flares (15% each), 3 cloth (1 in 15), the five charms (5% each, a quarter in all) and six anomalous pieces, one for every slot but the bag (the shard-lined cap and NAPO's crew hood, crew jacket, crew gloves, crew trousers and crew boots, white and taped at the seams, each keeping out some of what the anomalies do), 1 in 120 each and 1 in 20 in all. The quirks, rolled on any anomalous piece like the others: **hush** (skulkers hear you walking only from 4 steps, not 6), **lodestone** (while a shard lies within 5 steps, the piece's name pulses softly on the status panel and a faint double beat sounds, again every few seconds while one stays near, never saying which way; only your own game feels it) and **afterglow** (a flash discharging within 4 steps of you leaves you glowing faintly for 30 seconds, which everyone on the map sees, and the watchers keep off you meanwhile).

Why: the deep, restless run is where luck lives (farther is harder and richer), and with 7 outcomes its surprises run out in the first days.
