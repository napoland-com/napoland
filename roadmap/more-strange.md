Looking at a strange object in town shows one of 15 things instead of 7: two new charms (an ember coal, and fires warm you 20% faster; a pale moth, and crushing a glowcap gives you 1 energy), rare anomalous gear for every slot, which can be the kit of NAPO's crews in white suits, and now and then a handful of cloth. The rare gear together is 1 strange object in 20; charms stay about 1 in 4. Revealed gear lands in the bag, as it does today, and can be worn at once ([gear-on-the-road](gear-on-the-road.md)). Three new quirks come after.

Why: the deep, restless run is where luck lives (farther is harder and richer), and with 7 outcomes its surprises run out in the first days.
