The sign said Pop. 23, and in town there were only Mira and Walt, by the lodge fire. Now the town changes with what the whole server does. Edith, who stayed behind with Ruth in the NAPO Bunker when the others left, waits there until the Old Stone wakes, then comes home and lights a fire in the empty house next to yours: it becomes Edith's house, on its banner, its mailbox and the paper map, and the cloth it held moves to the lodge's shelves. Once enough fires were fed out there, Arvid comes back to light the sawmill's stove; once the town has thanked each other enough, Maud cooks at the lodge. Each is chalked over the number on the town's sign. Walt has four scenes that open as you level up and read his notes, and Edith two once she is home, kept in the journal's new People part; people have lines for the sky and for what you did and the town came to. Walt swaps surplus: 10 glowcaps for a cloth, 5 scrap for a road flare. A ledger in the lodge lists what each broken part of town needs (the street lights on the south road, a roof over the notice board, the sawmill's roof), given by anyone, each with a lasting perk for everyone, and the notice board shows how far each has come.

Why: home was a stash and a corridor; coming back should feel different as the game goes on. Stardew's Community Center and heart events.
