The sign says Pop. 23, and one person lives there. Two or three more people move in (the lineman at the lodge, an old woman back in the empty house), each with a few short scenes that open as you level and discover things, and lines that follow the sky and what you have done. One of them swaps surplus (10 glowcaps for a cloth, 5 scrap for a flare). The town changes with milestones the whole server reaches (every fifth time the Old Stone wakes, a dark house lights up and the population on the sign goes up), and a ledger in the lodge lists what each broken part of town needs, Stardew-style bundles with a lasting perk each (the ranger radio warns of storms 2 minutes early).

Why: home is a stash and a corridor today; coming back should feel different as the game goes on. Stardew's Community Center and heart events.
