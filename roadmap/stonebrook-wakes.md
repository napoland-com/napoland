The sign says Pop. 23, and in town there are only Mira and Walt, by the lodge fire. Walt gets his scenes, and Edith, who stayed behind with Ruth in the NAPO Bunker when the others left, comes home and lights a fire in the empty house again: it becomes Edith's house, and the cloth it held moves to the lodge's shelves. Each of them has a few short scenes that open as you level up and read the notes they are in, and lines that change with the sky and with what you have done. Walt also swaps surplus: 10 glowcaps for a cloth, 5 scrap for a road flare. Later, the town changes with milestones the whole server reaches (a dark house lights up, and the number on the sign goes up), and a ledger in the lodge lists what each broken part of town needs, each with a lasting perk.

Why: home is a stash and a corridor today; coming back should feel different as the game goes on. Stardew's Community Center and heart events.
