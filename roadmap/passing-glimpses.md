---
id: passing-glimpses
title: Other people's steps
status: idea
order: 160
depends: [survival-dynamics]
area: social
---

When you are alone on a map, every minute or two a pale figure in someone's jacket color walks 20 to 40 real steps somebody took there in the last day: to finds, to fires, home. It fades if you come within 3 tiles.

Why: with few players online, the woods feel empty; routes are learned by watching. Dark Souls' phantoms, extending the echoes already built. Keeping steps for a day is new data about players, so the privacy page changes with it.
