title: Other people's steps

When you are the only one on a map in the wilds, every minute or two a see-through figure in someone's color walks 20 to 40 real steps that somebody took there in the last day, on their way to a find, a fire or home. It shows no name, and it fades if you come within 3 tiles. Keeping steps for a day is new data about players, so the privacy page changes with it.

Why: with few players online the woods feel empty, and routes are learned by watching. Dark Souls' phantoms, extending the echoes already built.
