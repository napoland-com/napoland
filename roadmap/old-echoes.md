The see-through figure that walks a collapsed player's last steps also walks hand-written routes, at fixed times on the wall clock, for everyone at once. At dusk it is Walt's walk up the line the night the woods lit up, from pole 7 to the cabin at the end (he collapsed and woke at home, which is what an echo shows; he is alive, by the lodge fire). On aurora nights a child from the end cabin walks down to the pond, and on the South Road the leavers walk from their camp to the barrier. Follow one all the way and it leaves you something, once: a note, a warm pebble. Players learn the timetable and share it.

Why: a reason to be somewhere at a certain time, in a world that runs on a schedule you learn. Outer Wilds, Majora's Mask. It reuses what is built.
