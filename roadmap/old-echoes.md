The echo figure that replays a collapse walks hand-written routes too, at fixed times on the wall clock: at dusk the lineman walks from pole 7 to the end cabin; on aurora nights a child walks to the pond. Follow one to the end and it leaves a note or a charm, once per player. Players learn the timetable and share it ("be at the cross at :40").

Why: a reason to go out at a certain time, in a world that runs on a schedule you learn. Outer Wilds, Majora's Mask. Reuses what is built.
