A tool made at the workbench (2 wire, 1 scrap) that sits in the bag's header. Out there it crackles louder near shards, strange objects and live finds (loud within 6 tiles, faint within 15) and goes silent in a storm. It never shows a direction or a place: you search hot and cold.

Why: searching deep becomes a skill instead of a stroll, with no marker. Pacific Drive's scanners.
