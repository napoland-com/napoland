title: A crate that needs two
status: idea
order: 170
depends: [friends-messages]
area: social
---

On one fixed tile in the deep half, once per restless window, a sealed crate appears. It opens only when two players face it and press A within 3 seconds of each other; each gets two strange objects and a shard. The notice board says when one is out.

Why: nothing in the game needs two people yet. This is the smallest real test of "together you go farther", before group gates and shared light in [group-mechanics](group-mechanics.md).
