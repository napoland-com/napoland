In the middle of the ring of stones, where the rocks hum back, lies a flat slab. Each time the woods grow restless, seams glow along it like the Old Stone: it is the stones' own sealed crate, and it opens only for two. Two players face it and press A within 3 seconds of each other, and each gets two strange objects and a shard. The notice board says when it glows.

Why: nothing in the game needs two people yet. This is the smallest real test of "together you go farther", before the group gates and shared light of [group-mechanics](group-mechanics.md).
