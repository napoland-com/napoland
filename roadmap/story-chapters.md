A story every player goes through, chapter by chapter, and a journal in the menu that keeps the chapters you reached. Each chapter is reached by something the game already asks of you: bring something home, walk into the old cabin, feed a fire out in the wilds, talk to Tom, Ruth or Vera, read NAPO's notices and logs, give the Old Stone a shard. People hint at what comes next in their own words, and what they hint changes as your story moves on. The first chapters teach the game (the stash and XP, fires and energy, fires that burn down unless someone feeds them); the later ones go down the South Road to what NAPO did.

Why: new players learn the loop from the world instead of a tutorial, and the story of NAPO and Napoland ([the design](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-story-napo-and-napoland)) gets told in order, one place and one person at a time. It stays true to the pillars: no missions, nothing timed, rewarded or failed, no task list and no markers, and ignoring it costs nothing. There is no percentage and nothing to finish: new chapters are added at the end as the world grows, and whoever reached the end reads on from there.
