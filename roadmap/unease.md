Alone in the dark, away from any light, a feeling builds that you are not alone. There is no bar: the edges of the screen close in, you hear footsteps that are not yours, and on maps with watchers something sometimes stands at the edge of the fog, too far to make out, gone when you face it. It builds in about 3 minutes, faster with a watcher near or after a flash; once it is full, hitchhikers find you twice as often. A burning fire, a street light, a flare or another player within 4 tiles brings it down, company fastest. Tall grass hides you from creatures ([richer-places](richer-places.md)), but not from this.

Why: dread without more drain, and a reason just to walk with someone. Sunless Sea's terror, Darkwood.
