A hidden bar fills while you are alone in the dark away from light (full in about 3 minutes), faster near watchers or after a flash. As it rises the edges of the screen close in, you hear steps that are not there, and a figure stands sometimes at the edge of the fog. At full, hitchhikers find you twice as often. A fire, a street light, a flare or another player within 4 tiles brings it down.

Why: dread without more drain, and a reason just to walk with someone. Sunless Sea's terror, Darkwood.
