Time away from the game fills a cup of rest: 1 XP every 20 minutes you are not playing, up to 3 days' worth (216 XP), guests too. While the cup holds anything, what you stash earns double, and the extra comes out of the cup; parcels, lockboxes and anything else that earns nothing never touch it. The status panel shows how much is left, the chest says it, the XP over your head says the doubled part, and a banner says so when you arrive rested. Rest rewards time away, so skipping days costs nothing here; the daily parcels ([daily-parcels](daily-parcels.md)) reward coming back often; neither ever takes anything away.

Why: a reason to come back that is a gift, never a penalty for playing a lot, and it suits short phone sessions. World of Warcraft's rested XP.
