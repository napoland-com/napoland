Time away fills a cup: 1 XP every 20 minutes, up to 3 days' worth. While it holds anything, stashing earns double until it is empty. The status panel shows it.

Why: a reason to come back with no streak to break: skipping three days costs nothing. World of Warcraft's rested XP, framed as a bonus, never a penalty.
