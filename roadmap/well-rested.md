Time away from the game fills a cup of rest: 1 XP every 20 minutes you are not playing, up to 3 days' worth. While the cup holds anything, what you stash earns double, and the extra comes out of the cup. The status panel shows how much is left. Rest rewards time away, so skipping days costs nothing here; the daily parcels ([daily-parcels](daily-parcels.md)) reward coming back often; neither ever takes anything away.

Why: a reason to come back that is a gift, never a penalty for playing a lot, and it suits short phone sessions. World of Warcraft's rested XP.
