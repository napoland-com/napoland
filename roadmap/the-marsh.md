A region at depth 3 east of the Far Woods, along the loggers' corduroy road out of their old camp, open to anyone: the step between the Far Woods and the Burn, which takes two. Bog where the town cut its peat before the lights came: drowned trees, reeds, a mist that never lifts, and one shelter, the peat cutters' hut, whose fire nobody keeps.

What is new there:

- **Boardwalks built together.** Two channels cut the bog. The long way goes round them; across each stands a boardwalk that the whole server mends and keeps up (like the pond's footbridge): the west one of scrap, the east one of copper wire. With both standing, the black pool at the Marsh's heart is 30 steps nearer.
- **Lights on the water.** Pale lights drift over the channels and the pools, swelling and fading, the same for everyone. They lead nowhere; the cutters' board says to keep to the road.
- **Peat.** Bricks of it by the cuttings and in the cutters' stack: a fuel that burns longer than anything else a fire takes, so an armful carried out keeps a shelter fire going all night for whoever comes next.

Why: together you go farther, even when nobody is online at the same time (the boardwalks stand because many people gave to them), and a player not ready for the Burn has somewhere to go next. Farther is harder and richer: the Marsh is as deep as the Burn, with fewer shards, but peat nobody else has.
