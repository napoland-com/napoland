Cloth turns up outdoors in the Near Woods, by the cars, barrels and cabins, the way scrap does (4 at a time, back in 5 to 10 minutes), and each room that holds cloth has 2 at a time. With the South Road's dormitory and stores, that is about 44 an hour for the whole server instead of about 8: a raincoat (8 cloth) in two good trips, a mend (2 to 4) in one.

Why: gear is the main thing to work toward, and 10 of the 12 recipes need cloth, as does every mend below anomalous. With cloth only in a few rooms, one piece at a time, gear was out of reach as soon as a few people played. A change to `content/items.json` only.
