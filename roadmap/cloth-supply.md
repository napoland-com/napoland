Cloth grows today only in three rooms, one piece at a time, every 20 to 90 minutes: about 4 an hour for the whole server. Yet 10 of the 12 recipes need it (a raincoat 8, an expedition pack 16), and mending takes 2 to 4 a piece. Cloth also turns up outdoors in the Near Woods, by cars, barrels and houses, the way scrap does (4 at a time, back in 5 to 10 minutes), and each room holds 2. The aim is about 40 an hour server-wide: a raincoat in two good trips, a mend in one.

Why: gear is the main thing to work toward, and today it is out of reach as soon as a few people play. A change to `content/items.json` only.
