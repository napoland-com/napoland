Stonebrook's north road leads into the Near Woods, the first stretch of wilds: the old road, a creek ford, the pond, an old cabin, a bog, a ring of stones and a lit cabin deep in. Maps join at their edges like FireRed's towns and routes, and each greets you with its name. Out in the wilds your energy drains, faster the farther you are from home and in the rain. At zero you collapse and wake up at home.

Why: this is the heart of the game. Leave home, and get back before you run out; farther is harder.
