Farther regions do not come with a map. Each has its map torn in 4 pieces hidden across it; the pieces you found show on the paper and the rest stays blank. At a burning fire, charcoal lets you ink in the paths you walked, and you can draw up to 8 symbols of your own. It still never shows where you are.

Why: the paper map says farther maps are found or earned; this is how. The Long Dark's charcoal, Hollow Knight's quill.
