Every area so far has its map, and the Far Woods' map (depth 2) is found whole. From depth 3 on, a region's paper map is torn in 4 pieces hidden across it: tucked into a crate, under a rock, in a wreck. The pieces you found show on the paper and the rest stays blank with torn edges; like every paper map, it never shows where you are. Later, charcoal from a burning fire lets you ink in the paths you walked, and you can draw up to 8 symbols of your own.

Why: the design says maps of farther regions are found; this is how. The Long Dark's charcoal, Hollow Knight's quill.
