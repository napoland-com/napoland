At someone else's pile, A asks what to do: take half, as ever, or carry it to the lodge for them. Carried, the whole pile is tied up into a bundle ("Ana's things") that takes one bag slot and weighs what it holds, and the pile's hour stops. A bundle is never opened, traded, left in a crate, stashed or thrown away. Hand it in at the lost and found box in Stonebrook Lodge, a battered wooden box with a hand-lettered sign at Walt's elbow, who keeps what the leavers never came back for: the owner gets everything back in their chest, each piece of gear as it was, online or not, and their letter says "Bo carried what you lost by the pond back to the lodge." The carrier gets a quarter of the XP it was worth, the owner the rest, never more than it was worth all told (what the owner had taken out of their stash earns nobody anything, so it cannot be farmed), and the owner thanks the carrier. Collapse carrying one and it falls into your own pile, still a bundle, for anyone to carry on. Guests carry and hand in too.

Why: a stranger's collapse becomes a chance to help instead of a small loot. Death Stranding's lost cargo.
