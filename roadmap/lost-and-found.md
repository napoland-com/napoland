At someone else's pile, A asks what to do: take half, as today, or carry it to the lodge for them. Carried, the whole pile packs into a bundle that takes one bag slot and weighs what it holds, and the pile's hour stops. Hand it in at the lost and found box in the lodge, by Walt, who keeps what the leavers never came back for: the owner gets everything back in their chest, online or not, and you get a quarter of the XP it was worth (taken from theirs, so it cannot be farmed) and their thanks. Collapse with it and it falls into your own pile.

Why: a stranger's collapse becomes a chance to help instead of a small loot. Death Stranding's lost cargo.
