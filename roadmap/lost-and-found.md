At someone else's pile you can take half, as today, or pack it as a lost bundle: one slot, its full weight, and the pile's hour stops while you carry it. Hand it in at the lodge and the owner gets everything back in their chest; you get a quarter of its XP (taken from theirs, so it cannot be farmed) and a thanks. Collapse with it and it falls in your own pile.

Why: a stranger's collapse becomes a chance to help instead of a small loot. Death Stranding's lost cargo.
