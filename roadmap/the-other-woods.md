A region at depth 3, on past the ranger's camp: the Near Woods again, every path and pond of it, but the wrong way round, east for west, and always under the green sky of the night of the answer. Its cabins stand as burned shells, its signs read backwards, the lamp with no wires is lit, the pole tags read 61-N. A player who knows the Near Woods by heart finds their way by it, mirrored.

What is new there:

- **A sky that never moves.** It is always the night of the answer over the Other Woods: an aurora night that never ends, so what shows only at night or on a green night always shows there, and the dark wears you down as it does at night.
- **What the woods heard.** The ranger's last notes say what she made of it: not a place, but what the woods heard that night, said back. What it means is left open.

Why: farther is harder and richer, and the deepest places should turn what you learned on its head. One fixed world you learn (pillar 5): the copy rewards knowing the original.
