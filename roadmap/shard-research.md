Shards take after what made them: a calm shard (the deep finds), a restless shard (surges), a storm shard (storms). A desk in the lodge spends them on a small tree of 15 to 20 steps that opens tools, higher recipes and cheaper mending; some steps also need a page found in a deep cabin.

Why: each kind of hazard gets a reason of its own to go out. Pacific Drive's fabricator. Changes a written decision (every recipe is known from the start today), so it is the owner's call.
