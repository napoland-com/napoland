Shards take after what made them: a calm shard lies deep in the woods and at NAPO's field site all the time (today's anomaly shard), a restless shard turns up while the woods are restless before a surge, and a storm shard by the old poles while a storm blows. A research desk in the NAPO Laboratory, Vera's, spends them (paid from the bag, since the stash is at home) on a small tree of steps that opens new workbench recipes and cheaper mending. Every recipe known today stays open to everyone, and anomalous gear still only comes from strange objects.

Why: each kind of hazard gets a reason of its own to go out. Pacific Drive's fabricator.
