The Far Woods' thread is the trappers, who always worked in pairs. Two sets of notes lie at the two ends of the trail, one by each of the pair, written to the other: one in the trapper's cabin west of the ford, one at the field post in the hollow. Read either and it is half of a quarrel and half of a plan; read both, in either order, and a page of your field notes says what the two of them agreed to do the week before the answer, and that one of them went up to the post alone. The cabin's tally and the post's log already say the rest.

Why: the region asks you to go in pairs, and this gives it a story about why. Pillar 4, together you go farther.
