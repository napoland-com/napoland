Places in the Near Woods you walk past on your first trips and cannot use yet: a padlocked shed behind the ranger's hut, which needs bolt cutters, and a flooded culvert by the bog that comes out south of the creek, which needs waders. Both tools are made at the workbench at home from what you bring back from deep in, and sit in the bag's header for good. Behind the shed door a strange object grows back every 30 minutes; the culvert is a way home from the bog that saves about 44 steps. A pitch-dark root cellar and a lantern come later.

Why: nothing in the woods is "for later" today: everything can be reached on the first trip. Subnautica's depth-gated tools, in a fixed world you learn.
