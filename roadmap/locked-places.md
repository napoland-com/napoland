title: Places you can see but not reach yet
status: idea
order: 40
depends: [equipment-resistances]
area: world
---

Each region has two or three hand-placed places closed until you have the right tool: a flooded culvert (waders), a padlocked ranger shed (bolt cutters), a pitch-dark root cellar (a lantern). Tools sit in the bag's header like the paper map and are made at the workbench from deep materials. Behind each is something found nowhere else: a sure strange object that grows back every 30 minutes, a shortcut home, a note.

Why: nothing in the woods is "for later" today. Subnautica's depth-gated tools, in a fixed world you learn.
