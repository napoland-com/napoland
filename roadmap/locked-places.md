Places in the Near Woods you walk past on your first trips and cannot use yet: a padlocked shed behind the ranger's hut, which needs bolt cutters, and a flooded culvert by the bog that comes out south of the creek, in the headlight clearing, which needs waders. Both tools are made at the workbench at home from what you bring back from deep in (shards and wire), and sit in the bag's header for good. Behind the shed door a strange object grows back every 30 minutes, the same one for everyone; the culvert takes you from the bog to the south bank in 44 steps instead of 88 round by the ford. Both are on the paper map. Pulled at without its tool, the shed's door says so in the words of NAPO's gate into the Burn, which will not move for one: it gives a little, and no more.

Why: nothing in the woods was "for later": everything could be reached on the first trip. Subnautica's depth-gated tools, in a fixed world you learn.

Later: a pitch-dark root cellar, and a lantern to see in it.
