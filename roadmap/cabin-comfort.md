Your own cabin ([own-cabin](own-cabin.md)) has six places whose furniture years of damp spoiled: a stove, a bed, a rug, a lamp, a drying rack and a trophy shelf, each standing there spoiled (a cold rusted stove, a bare bed frame, a rotted rug, a cracked lamp, a broken rack, an empty warped shelf) until you make it again. Each is made at the workbench at home from scrap, cloth, wire and resin, dearer the more comfort it adds, and set in its place at once, never in the stash: the stove 3, the bed and the rack 2, the rug, the lamp and the shelf 1, 10 in all. Only you see them. Stand by your own fire for 20 seconds and you are **cozy**: out in the wilds you tire 10% slower for 5 minutes, plus a minute for each point of comfort, counted from when you leave the fire (the status panel counts it down; a collapse ends it). With the rack in place you always leave the cabin dry; the shelf shows the charms and anomalous gear your stash holds, for when guests come.

Why: home upgrades give your own cabin a shape, and scrap, cloth and wire keep a use once you have your gear. Valheim's comfort.
