Your own cabin ([own-cabin](own-cabin.md)) has six empty places, since years of damp spoiled what the leavers left in it: a stove, a bed, a rug, a lamp, a drying rack and a trophy shelf. Each is made at the workbench at home and set in its place, and each adds comfort, up to 10 in all. Stand by your own fire for 20 seconds and you are **cozy**: out in the wilds you tire 10% slower for 5 minutes, plus a minute for each point of comfort. With the rack in place you always leave the cabin dry; the shelf will show charms and anomalous gear to guests.

Why: home upgrades are in the design but have no shape yet, and scrap, cloth and wire run out of uses once you have your gear. Valheim's comfort.
